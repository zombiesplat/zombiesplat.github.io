# From The Pressbox Errata: Drum Corps Scores, 1973–2012

[From The Pressbox](TODO-link) has the most complete record of drum corps scores
from before the internet. I'm grateful it exists, and without it [DCI Scores](https://dciscores.com)
would have no history before 1992 at all.

When I imported DCI and DCA scores from 1972 through 2012, I reviewed one season at a time
and checked every show against the tours of the corps in it. Along the way I found
errors in the source pages. Most of them are the kind of mistake anyone makes when
retyping thousands of score sheets by hand. This page lists every one I found so the record
can be corrected, and so the next person who works with this data doesn't have to find
them all again.

In total:

- **41 recaps repeat another show's sheet**, with every total and caption score
  copied row for row under other corps' names.
- **5 recaps carry another show's Total column**, while their captions still add up to the real Sub Totals.
- **21 recap rows have the wrong corps**, which the season table and the tours show.
- Plus wrong dates, two shows run together on one sheet, and some spelling mistakes.

### How to read this

*Recap pages* are From The Pressbox's monthly recap documents (e.g. `1999julyrecaps.htm`).
The *season table* is the final-scores table on each season's page. Each entry says what
the page shows, how I can tell it's wrong (usually because a corps' score that night
doesn't fit its scores in the days around it), and what DCI Scores did with it.
When a show is a copy, its real scores are unknown, so DCI Scores leaves it out instead of guessing.

If you have the original sheets for any of these shows, I'd love to hear from you.

## 1973: A 1972 Show Repeated

- **Milwaukee, WI 6/15/1973:** the 1973 season table repeats 1972's Milwaukee show on
  the same date, word for word (Madison Scouts 72.8, Black Knights 71.35, Kilties
  69.55, Racine Scouts 54.1, Americanos 49.15). It belongs to 1972: its Black Knights
  (a Wisconsin corps) tour all of 1972 and have no other 1973 show.
